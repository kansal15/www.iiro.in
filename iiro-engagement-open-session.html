The Question for the Room

Who Gets the First Job in the Age of AI?

As AI automates the entry-level work that young people in India and Nepal rely on to start their careers, what policy interventions, by whom and at which level, can protect the first job and prepare young people for the work that replaces it?

For two decades, millions of young people in both countries began their careers in call centres, data entry, junior coding and accounts. Generative AI now does much of that work fastest and cheapest. Companies are often not firing people; they are quietly not hiring the next batch.

This session asks young people what should be done, by whom, and at which level: local, national, regional or global.
